# Source Notes — Arabic Concept PDF

This file normalizes the attached Arabic PDF into compact engineering context so Codex does not need to parse the PDF during routine work.

## Problem described

The concept addresses a communication gap in care:
- stroke patients may have difficulty speaking despite full awareness
- ICU patients may be unable to express themselves because of ventilation
- older adults and people with low vision may struggle with complex smartphones
- alternatives such as gestures or pen/paper may be tiring or unclear
- inability to request basic needs can cause frustration/isolation
- families may remain anxious when absent

## Physical concept

The source proposes:
- approximately A5 size
- about 250 g
- approximately 7–9 mm
- portable / bedside-friendly
- large white writing area
- quick symbol buttons
- thick easy-grip stylus
- stylus tether/retention
- magnetic fixation concept
- calm white/navy medical visual identity
- avoid childish/rainbow styling

## UI concept

The source includes:
- free writing area
- black / blue / red / green writing
- large basic-need symbols
- lock protection
- clear action
- audible speech from written/selected communication

The visual mockup additionally shows yes/no-style controls, temperature/cold-style symbols, nurse/family actions, and an emergency-call-style control.

## Accessibility concept

Three modes:
1. standard visual
2. low vision: larger symbols, higher contrast, illuminated/bounded controls, audio and vibration
3. blind users: tactile/mechanical controls, tactile symbols, full speech, confirmation vibration

For Android, mechanical/tactile hardware becomes TalkBack + haptics + semantic/focus support until dedicated hardware exists.

## Family app concept

The source proposes:
- timestamped communication event history
- no continuous audio recording
- smart alerts when Pain repeats (example: twice in one hour)
- a main owner account able to invite family
- immediate synchronization of written messages and selected symbols
- secure Bluetooth connection

For the Android MVP, owner/family structure is preserved as a requirement, but cloud accounts are deferred unless explicitly approved.

## Original cost concept

The PDF contains rough initial hardware cost/sale ranges for:
- simple LCD version
- smart/hybrid Bluetooth + Family app + voice version
- advanced ICU/cellular version

These are concept estimates only, not verified quotations.

## Original rollout

The PDF proposes:
1. simple LCD test
2. smart Bluetooth prototype
3. evaluation with speech/nursing specialists
4. pilot production of approximately 50–100 units

Current project decision supersedes the immediate order:
**Android app first**, hardware later.
